Phishing Email Detection and Awareness

1. Project Objective

The objective of this project is to understand phishing emails and identify common warning signs that may indicate an email is fraudulent.

2. Introduction

Phishing is a cyberattack in which attackers use fake emails or messages to trick people into revealing sensitive information, such as passwords and banking details.

3. Tools Used

- GitHub – to store and submit project files.
- Web browser – to study phishing examples.
- Text editor – to prepare the report.

4. Methodology

1. Study sample email messages.
2. Examine the sender address and subject line.
3. Look for urgent language and suspicious links.
4. Identify requests for passwords or financial information.
5. Classify each sample as suspected phishing or likely legitimate.
6. Record observations and safety recommendations.

5. Sample Email Analysis

Sample 1

Subject: Urgent! Your account will be suspended.

Message: The recipient is asked to click a link and enter their password to prevent account suspension.

Warning signs:

- Creates urgency.
- Requests a password.
- Contains an unverified link.

Classification: Suspected phishing.

Sample 2

Subject: College assignment update.

Message: An expected assignment is sent from a verified college email address.

Observations: The sender and context appear familiar, but attachments and links must still be checked.

Classification: Likely legitimate; verification is required.

Sample 3

Subject: Congratulations! You won a prize.

Message: The recipient is asked to pay a fee to claim a prize they never entered to win.

Warning signs:

- Unexpected prize.
- Requests payment.
- Uses a potentially deceptive offer.

Classification: Suspected phishing.

6. Safety Recommendations

- Never share passwords or OTPs through email links.
- Check the sender's full email address.
- Avoid clicking unexpected links and attachments.
- Visit official websites directly to verify messages.
- Report suspicious emails to the relevant organisation.

7. Conclusion

This project demonstrates how common warning signs can help users recognise suspected phishing emails. Careful verification and cybersecurity awareness can reduce the risk of online fraud.

Note: The examples in this report are educational samples, not emails collected from a real investigation.
